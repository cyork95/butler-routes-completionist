## Borderline

### (a) 94817 Confounding Flash vs 94824 Confounding Flash — RECOMMEND: 94824 IN, 94817 OUT
Both are level-10 Gnome Priest quests with the same name and no pre-link, mutually exclusive (each lists the other in excl). They are alternative versions of one quest: 94824 (Maxan Anvol to High Priestess Mims) continues the Kharanos priest chain 5626-5625, while 94817 (Mims to Mims) is the standalone variant. Only one can ever complete, so only one can sit in the contract. Recommendation: contract 94824 (chain-consistent), keep 94817 OUT as the unpicked variant. If the pilot shows Gnome Priests are actually offered 94817 first, swap them.

Both are required by the pre-chain, so both are IN, routed in chain order; the Dwarf playtest will prove whether 287 stays available after 282. If the site lint flags it, the guide phase handles it.
